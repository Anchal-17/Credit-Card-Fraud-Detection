📊 Dataset Information

The dataset used in this project is **Credit Card Fraud Detection**, originally available on **Kaggle**.

Due to its large size, it is **not included in this repository**.

You can download it from the following source:

🔗 Download Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud

After downloading, extract or copy the file `creditcard.csv` into this folder.
